Algebra steps

This is a test of typing algebra steps on a screen. It is for adults aged 18 or over.

You will work through ten short equations, one line at a time. Each line is checked when you press Check.

What you type goes to the server to be checked and is not saved there. The hosting service can see your IP address and your steps. At the end you choose whether to send a summary, which holds counts and the shape of any lines that failed, not what you typed.
